## 📖 Overview

Fitness and personal wellness have never been more talked about — yet most people still struggle to find guidance that's actually *personalized* and *accessible*. Popular apps like BodBot or Home Workout (No Equipment) hand out the same plans to everyone, regardless of medical history, injuries, or fitness level.

**Virtual Fitness Trainer** is built to close that gap. It's a fitness training system designed to:

- Generate **personalized, free workout and diet plans** based on a user's health profile
- **Track and visualize progress** over time
- Adapt plans for users with **health conditions or physical limitations**
- Offer **professional tips** to improve fitness level, form, and body composition

The long-term goal is a system that feels less like a static app and more like a trainer who actually knows your body.

## ✨ Planned Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🧍 **Personalized Plans** | Workout and diet recommendations tailored to age, weight, goals, and health conditions |
| 📊 **Progress Tracking** | Log workouts and monitor improvements over time |
| 🩺 **Health-Aware Recommendations** | Plans adjusted for users with injuries or medical considerations — not just "healthy by default" users |
| 💡 **Coaching Tips** | Actionable guidance to improve form, consistency, and results |
| 💸 **Free to Use** | Core features accessible without a paywall |
